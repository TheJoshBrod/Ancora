# References

Papers and prior work relevant to Ancora. PDFs are kept locally and are
**not committed** (see `.gitignore`); each entry links to the public source.

| Paper | Link | Local file | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Donahue, Lipton & McAuley, *Dance Dance Convolution* (ICML 2017) | [arXiv:1703.06891](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.06891) · [code](https://github.com/chrisdonahue/ddc) | `1703.06891v3_dance_dance_convolution.pdf` | DDR charts: C-LSTM step placement + LSTM step selection, difficulty-conditioned. Mirroring augmentation; song-grouped split; easy charts much harder to place. |
| Waissbluth, Carr, Popescu & Hu, *Tensor Hero: Generating Playable Guitar Hero Charts from Any Song* (UC Berkeley capstone, 2022) | [report](https://www.ischool.berkeley.edu/sites/default/files/sproject_attachments/tensorhero_capstone.pdf) | `tensorhero_capstone.pdf` | 450 rock songs; end-to-end Transformer did not converge. Final: NINOS onset detection + a model predicting note "contour" (chord shape + motion). Markov difficulty conversion. |
| Défossez, Copet, Synnaeve & Adi, *High Fidelity Neural Audio Compression* (EnCodec, 2022) | [arXiv:2210.13438](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.13438) · [code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/encodec) | `2210.13438v1_encodec.pdf` | Neural codec: 75 frames/s at 24 kHz, RVQ up to 32 codebooks × 1,024. 6 kbps = 8 codebooks. Built for reconstruction, not music semantics. |
| Yi, Lee & Lee, *Beat-Aligned Spectrogram-to-Sequence Generation of Rhythm-Game Charts* (ISMIR LBD 2023) | [arXiv:2311.13687](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.13687) · [demos/code](https://stet-stet.github.io/goct) | `2311.13687v1_beat_aligned_charts.pdf` | osu!mania 4k. Spectrogram hop = 1/48 beat; time (0-95) + action tokens. Beat alignment lifts triplet F1 from 0.09 to 0.59. Small Transformer (3 layers, d=256). CC BY 4.0. |
| Tripodi, *audio2chart: End to End Audio Transcription into playable Guitar Hero charts* (2025) | [arXiv:2511.03337](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.03337) · [code](https://github.com/3podi/audio2chart) · [models](https://huggingface.co/3podi) | `2511.03337v1_audio2chart.pdf` | EnCodec + Transformer decoder with cross-attention, 63 fret-combination tokens on a 20/40 ms grid, ~10k charts (all difficulties). Trains on 30 s chunks; pad loss down-weighted; encoder frozen. |
| Grohotolski, Oseili & Pleman, *Tab Hero: Turning Songs into Gameplay* (U. Michigan SIADS 696, 2026) | [repo](https://github.com/MattGroho/tab-hero) · [model](https://huggingface.co/MattGroho/tab-hero) | `tab_hero_final_report.pdf` | ~100M mel-spectrogram Transformer, difficulty + instrument embeddings, 4-token notes (time delta, frets, modifier, duration), ~50k charts, RTX 5090. Repo has no license. |
| Opria, *STRUM: A Spectral Transcription and Rhythm Understanding Model for End-to-End Generation of Playable Rhythm-Game Charts* (2026) | [arXiv:2605.12135](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12135) · [code](https://github.com/opria123/strum) · [weights](https://huggingface.co/opria123/strum) | `2605.12135v1.pdf` | Multi-stage, 5 instruments: HTDemucs 6-stem, onset CRNN + pYIN + rule-based lanes for guitar. Guitar onset F1 0.651 at ±100 ms. Community charts cap recall near 0.89. MIT. |
| thejorseman, *CloneCharter* (2026) | [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/thejorseman/CloneCharter) | none (model card only) | 8M encoder-decoder on per-beat MERT + log-mel; `BEAT_BOUNDARY` + `WAIT(1-48)` tokens; ~42,600 charts; needs BPM supplied. MIT. Closest to our v1. |
